INNOV8 / ABOUT

BUILT NAIROBI / KE IN NAIROBI. DESIGNED FOR THE WORLD.

Innov8 Studios is an independent creative technology studio working at the intersection of design, technology, marketing and visual storytelling.

We partner with businesses that are building something worth noticing — creating the identities, digital experiences, campaigns and visual systems that help them move forward.

Small enough to care about the details.
Serious enough to handle the big ones.

01

WHY INNOV8

There was a gap worth filling.

The creative industry has changed.

Businesses no longer need separate companies for every piece of a modern brand. The boundaries between design, technology, media, culture and business are disappearing.

Innov8 was created in response to that shift.

We believe the next generation of creative companies should be smaller, sharper, more technologically capable and considerably more ambitious.

Not built around bureaucracy.
Built around ideas.

02

WHAT WE BELIEVE

We believe creative companies can be businesses in their own right.

  • They can build intellectual property.
  • They can develop technology.
  • They can create brands.
  • They can enter new markets.
  • They can build products.
  • They can become institutions.

Innov8 is being built with that possibility in mind.

NAIROBI

Nairobi is our starting point.

Our starting point. Not our ceiling.

NAIROBI-1.2921, 36.8219 AFRICA THE WORLD

Nairobi is one of the reasons Innov8 exists.

It is a city where finance, technology, culture, entrepreneurship and creativity increasingly intersect.

It is also a city accustomed to solving difficult problems with limited resources — an environment that rewards people who can think differently and execute.

We are proud to build from here.
But we don't see geography as a ceiling.

THE AMBITION

We're not trying to build another agency.

The traditional agency model is not the only way a creative company can exist.

Innov8 is being built as something more fluid: part creative studio, part technology company, part intellectual property house, part platform for creative talent.

The exact composition will evolve.
The ambition won't.
